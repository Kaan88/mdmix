SoundBlaster X G6: GUI mockup for the Rust rewrite

One static mockup in the look of mdshared-ui (markdown-shared-rs). Every control is clickable; nothing talks to a real device. Sample state: firmware 2.1.250903.1324, Speakers, Direct mode on (so SBX is shown bypassed until you turn Direct mode off).

Pages worth a look

SBX Acoustic EngineThe SBX effects as cards, with the Direct mode notice.
EqualizerA curve with draggable points over the ten band sliders, 43 presets.
MixerA live console: channel strips with level meters, faders and mutes.
Signal chainThe sound's path as stages, with Direct mode as a bypass over SBX. A stage opens its page; the switches on it apply at once.
Playback and decoderDirect mode, decoder, DAC filter, and the speaker setup per output.

Design rule: every change applies at once

  • There is no preview mode and no apply step. A switch, a choice, a preset, a colour or a profile takes effect on the click.
  • Sliders and EQ points take effect while they are dragged (throttled), and send the final value on release.
  • A small status line says what changed ("Output: Headphones, volume 34") and offers Undo (also Ctrl+Z). The matching CLI flag is its tooltip.
  • A volume never jumps: drag its knob, or press beside it to move one step.
  • If a write is refused, the control springs back to the device's value and a short error shows. Try it: switch on "Writes fail" in the bar at the bottom, then change anything.
  • Without the G6 a banner says so and the device controls are disabled. Try it: switch off "G6 connected".
  • Speaker setup (Stereo / 5.1 / 7.1) is kept per output, Speakers and Headphones. On Windows it sets the speaker setup of the G6's playback endpoint.
